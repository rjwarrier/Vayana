# Unreleased

- Search: recognized PDF text is now included in library-wide Book contents search after recognition finishes. Results open the matching scan page; re-recognition and text removal invalidate the derived index.
- Bulk actions: Prepare offline queues durable book downloads with optional PDF recognition and a language-model choice. The queue shows waiting, working, ready and failed states, with cancel/retry. Recognition can resume saved pages; batch downloads and recognition are bounded to one book each to limit memory use.
- Privacy & data: view installed recognition-model sizes and recognized text by book, remove unused models or individual books’ derived text, and see OCR storage categories in Statistics. Active/queued models are protected. Clearing text stops related preparation/recognition and keeps the source book, highlights and reading position.
- Backups: imported font files, font selection and per-book pronunciation corrections are now included. Restore preview shows their counts. Older backups remain readable and keep existing device customizations; new backups require the updated app.
- Dictionaries: stop searching additional translation dictionaries once two distinct extra languages have been found, keeping the same preference order and fallback definitions.
- Text recognition: blank and page-number-only results no longer offer an empty Text view; empty-spine EPUBs generated by earlier versions are ignored.
- Reader: keeping the current position in a scanned book's Text view saves its printed PDF page; accepting a synced scan position opens that page in Text view. Scan-view progress stays unchanged.
- Reader: position prompt buttons always close the prompt, even before the printed page or text page map is available; a missing position skips only the save or jump.
- Text recognition: search reloads recognised text after words broken across lines are joined, including when the final page count stays the same. Concurrent downloads of the same language model use separate verified temporary files.
- Text recognition: abandoned model-download partial files older than 24 hours are removed when model storage opens; recent downloads and installed models are kept.
- Data usage: the sync history log trims to 512 KiB when it exceeds 1 MiB, avoiding a full rewrite after every later sync.
- Help: tips now cover the Statistics periods and chart switch, dictionaries in more languages, exact-phrase and scoped search, pronunciations, Data usage and Privacy & data, and correctly say a cover opens Book details and a double-tap opens the reader tools. In all nine app languages.
- Fix the Today card's Continue button picking another book when the one you're reading had been marked finished (by hand or a Goodreads import) and was then read again.
- Book details: Mark as reading takes a book marked finished before its end back to Reading, so its progress, time and finish-by plan show again.
- An EPUB or PDF counts as Reading only after 5 minutes of reading (on any synced device). Opening a book or leafing through its contents still saves your place, but no longer gives it a start date, a progress bar, a place in the Reading filter or the Continue cards; jumping to the last chapter no longer finishes it. Books already opened that way count as not started until read for 5 minutes.
- Quote cards open in a full-screen editor: a large live preview with Text (edit the quote; Serif, Sans or Mono for the quote and, separately, the book title; Light to Bold weight; italic; size, with Fit to card (on by default) shrinking a long quote until all of it shows; alignment), Layout (square, 4:5 portrait or 9:16 story, four layouts, space before the book details, an optional divider, backdrop opacity), Colors (the app's light or dark colours, or Paper, Sepia, Rose, Forest, Midnight and Ink) and Show tabs, and Share text / Share image always at the bottom. Add your own seal (a PNG, ideally a white shape on transparent) in Settings → Profile or the editor's Show tab: it sits at the top right of the card in the card's accent colour, with its own size slider and a crop screen (drag the frame, or Fit to shape to trim transparent margins). The editor remembers your last look across quotes and restarts. Images export at full resolution in the chosen shape.
- Book cards get the same full-screen editor: Text (per-image title, title font, weight, italic and size, a font for the author and stats), Layout (square, portrait or story, where the cover moves above the details, plus the four layouts and backdrop opacity), Colors (the same palettes) and Show (every detail chip, plus your seal and its size). The last look is remembered.
- Share cards: add your profile name in Settings → Profile and show it at the bottom right of book and quote cards (Profile name chip), with the tagline on the left. Book cards no longer show the imported or added date.

See [Vayana 0.93](v0.93.md) for the previously released changes since v0.92.
