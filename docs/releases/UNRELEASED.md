# Unreleased

- Help: tips now cover the Statistics periods and chart switch, dictionaries in more languages, exact-phrase and scoped search, pronunciations, Data usage and Privacy & data, and correctly say a cover opens Book details and a double-tap opens the reader tools. In all nine app languages.
- Fix the Today card's Continue button picking another book when the one you're reading had been marked finished (by hand or a Goodreads import) and was then read again.
- Book details: Mark as reading takes a book marked finished before its end back to Reading, so its progress, time and finish-by plan show again.
- An EPUB or PDF counts as Reading only after 5 minutes of reading (on any synced device). Opening a book or leafing through its contents still saves your place, but no longer gives it a start date, a progress bar, a place in the Reading filter or the Continue cards; jumping to the last chapter no longer finishes it. Books already opened that way count as not started until read for 5 minutes.
- Quote cards open in a full-screen editor: a large live preview with Text (edit the quote; Serif, Sans or Mono for the quote and, separately, the book title; Light to Bold weight; italic; size; alignment), Layout (square, 4:5 portrait or 9:16 story, four layouts, space before the book details, an optional divider, backdrop opacity), Colors (the app's light or dark colours, or Paper, Sepia, Rose, Forest, Midnight and Ink) and Show tabs, and Share text / Share image always at the bottom. Add your own seal (a PNG, ideally a white shape on transparent) in Settings → Profile or the editor's Show tab: it sits at the top right of the card in the card's accent colour, with its own size slider and a crop screen (drag the frame, or Fit to shape to trim transparent margins). The editor remembers your last look across quotes and restarts. Images export at full resolution in the chosen shape.
